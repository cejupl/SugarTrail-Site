Why we built it

Diabetes is a job nobody applied for, and most of it is invisible.

  1. 01

    The counting never stops. Every plate is arithmetic: carbs, doses, timing, all day, every day.

  2. 02

    The remembering. What you ate, what you took, what happened after - and retelling it all at the clinic three months later.

  3. 03

    The admin nobody sees. Refills, prior authorisations, supplies running out, checks and labs to keep track of.

  4. 04

    The pieces that never meet. A sensor app, a pump app, a meter, a portal - each holding one part of the story, none holding it all.

  5. 05

    The alone part. A hundred small decisions a day, and few people who really get it.

…and much more. We know.

What SugarTrail does

Here’s how.

  • Keeps your days in one place.

    The Trail: meals, doses, readings, sleep and device changes on one line. Log food with a photo; everything else is pulled from your sensor, pump, phone and clinic.

  • Shows you what works for you.

    Every food’s own history - what you did last time, what happened after. Patterns in your own numbers, tips that fit the moment, and a buddy you can ask: “what changed this week?”

  • Reminds you before anything slips.

    A sensor ending, a pod expiring, supplies running low, a check that’s due. A page ready for your next visit.

  • Puts people who understand within reach.

    A caregiver who sees what you choose. Communities for your type and situation. A report for your care team.

  • Keeps you in the know.

    Where diabetes science is heading, which clinical trials are recruiting and what they’ve found, and recalls for your exact devices - weekly, in the app and in your inbox.

Why it works

A lighter day. A clearer trail.

We built SugarTrail because the work of diabetes is real and mostly invisible. So the goal is plain: less of that work on you, a trail you can read, and someone walking it with you.

  1. Day one.

    Tell SugarTrail who it’s for, where your data already lives, and what you take. Add your clinic dates, invite whoever helps you, join the communities that fit, and pick up the weekly digest. Then a photo at each meal - that’s your whole job. SugarTrail offers the likely match, and what it knows about that food comes with it. None of this has to happen today, but each piece you connect is one the app never has to ask you for again.

  2. Week one.

    Now starts earning its keep: a sensor ending, a pod due, supplies running low, a check you’d forgotten. It speaks when something needs you and stays quiet when nothing does. The people you invited start seeing what you chose to share, the rooms you joined start answering, and the week’s diabetes news arrives already filtered to your type and your devices. SugarTrail is in it with you too - a small tip when it fits the moment, never a lecture.

  3. Month one.

    Your days have shape. The same lunch shows what it did last time, before you decide anything. Your effort gets noticed too - a month of medicines taken on time is worth saying out loud, so SugarTrail says it. The people who care about you can see the shape of your month instead of asking how you’ve been, and by now asking the app, or the room, has stopped feeling like a big deal.

  4. Month three.

    Your first visit with everything already gathered: what changed, what you asked last time, what’s worth raising. By now the whole of it works together - your people see what you choose, your rooms know your situation, the digest keeps you current, and the app knows your days well enough to be useful without being asked. And if something is missing or wrong, tell us. What’s genuine gets built, and you’ll hear back from a person.

  5. Trail forward.

    Three months in, you’re only beginning. From here the trail is the thing you keep: what tends to work for you, described plainly and never prescribed; the weight of remembering carried somewhere other than your head; and people who understand within reach on the days it’s heavy. The progress is yours. SugarTrail keeps it where you can see it.

The more you share, the more SugarTrail can give back.

Four promises

Rules in the code, not wishes.

  • Never asks twice.

    What your devices and other apps already know, SugarTrail pulls in. You never type it again.

  • Never decides for you.

    What you take, and when, stays between you and your clinician. SugarTrail brings your history to that conversation.

  • Calm by design.

    Only what needs you, when it needs you. The rest stays quiet.

  • Never shares without you.

    Your data leaves only when you send it. Export or delete all of it, any time.

Setting up takes a few minutes. Two answers are required; everything else can wait, and all of it can change later.

What’s next

The trail ahead.

SugarTrail is being built one honest piece at a time. Here’s what’s coming, in roughly the order it arrives.

  • Every source, one trail.

    Your sensor, your pump and your phone, connected one at a time - so you never type in something a device already knows.

  • The food memory.

    Photograph a meal once. The next time you eat it, SugarTrail knows what it is and what it did to you last time.

  • Rooms for the moment you’re in.

    Newly diagnosed, starting a pump, pregnancy, a trip, a hospital stay. Small groups that end when the moment does.

  • Parents, together.

    For parents of children with type 1, grouped by the age their child is now, because the questions change every year.

  • Labs and medicines, from your clinic.

    Your results and your prescription list, pulled in from your health record, so the numbers you are asked about are already there.

  • Android.

    SugarTrail starts on iPhone because that’s where the connections open first. Android follows.

None of this carries a date. We would rather ship each piece when it is right than promise you a month.
