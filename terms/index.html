What SugarTrail is

SugarTrail is a personal record-keeping companion for people living with diabetes and those who care for them. It gathers information you already have - from your devices, from apps you use, and from what you log - and shows it back to you in one place. That is the whole of it.

What SugarTrail is not

  • It is not a medical device and has not been cleared or approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration or any other regulator.
  • It does not provide medical advice. Settings you enter are shown back to you exactly as your clinic wrote them; the app never turns them into a recommended dose.
  • It is not an alert system. Data reaches the app minutes to hours after it is recorded. Your sensor's own app is what warns you, and SugarTrail never replaces it.
  • It is not a substitute for your doctor, nurse or diabetes educator.

Using the app responsibly
